Animation Scenario: My Memories

The program renders a 600 x 600 Java Swing animation that loops continuously every
18 seconds. The animation uses a hand-drawn stickman style and transitions between
the present and two childhood memories.

Timeline
--------

### 0.0-4.6 seconds: Present - night stargazing

The character lies on a grassy hill at night with his hands behind his head and his
feet pointing to the right. The scene contains a deep blue gradient sky, Milky Way,
moon, twinkling stars, distant mountains, swaying grass, and moonlit flowers. The
character's body gently moves to suggest breathing.

At 1.2-2.6 seconds, a shooting star crosses the sky. From 1.8-4.3 seconds, the
camera smoothly zooms toward the character's eye. The eyes begin closing at 3.2
seconds and are fully closed at 3.9 seconds.

At 4.6 seconds, a bright white warp/flash transition opens the first memory.

### 4.6-9.6 seconds: Memory 1 - football match

The scene changes to a grassy football field. A player runs beneath a falling ball,
leaps, and performs a bicycle kick at 1.95 seconds into the memory. The ball then
flies toward the goalkeeper, hits him, and carries him off toward the right side of
the screen. The impact includes speed lines, screen shake, a white flash, and impact
effects. After the play, the kicker performs a celebratory SIUUU pose.

At 9.6 seconds, another white warp/flash transition opens the second memory.

### 9.6-15.6 seconds: Memory 2 - toy sword fight

The scene takes place in a dramatic sunset battlefield with a purple-to-orange sky,
glowing sun, mountains, hills, embers, and dark foreground ground. Two children with
blue and red scarves face each other while holding glowing toy swords.

From 0.0-0.6 seconds into the memory, they charge energy in battle-ready stances.
From 0.6-1.4 seconds, they dash toward each other. From 1.4-3.0 seconds, they
exchange rapid toy-sword slashes with sparks, impact bursts, and screen shake.
At 3.0 seconds, one child hits the other with a downward swing, producing a comic
"BONK!" effect. From 4.2 seconds onward, the injured child cries with exaggerated
waterfall tears while the other child panics and apologizes.


### 15.6-15.6 seconds: Memory 3 - เล่นน้ำ

จะเปลี่ยนฉากไปเป็นตัวละครกำลังเล่นน้ำกับเพื่อนอีก 3 คนอยากสนุกสนาน หัวเราะด้วยกันอย่างมีความสุข



### 15.6-18.0 seconds: Return to the present

The animation warps back to the night stargazing scene. The character is still lying
on the grass with the camera initially close to the eye. From 16.2-17.6 seconds, the
camera smoothly zooms back out. The character's eyes reopen from 16.8-17.4 seconds,
making the return seamless before the 18-second loop starts again.

Transitions and visual effects
------------------------------

- White warp flashes occur when entering each memory and when returning to the present.
- Expanding circular rings originate near the character's head during each warp.
- The football impact and sword "BONK!" use separate short white flashes and screen shake.
- Stars twinkle, grass sways, and the present scene has a dark vignette around the edges.

Camera and presentation
-----------------------

- Fixed 600 x 600 viewport.
- Present scene: side-oriented view of the lying stickman, with a close-up zoom into
  the eye and a later zoom-out.
- Memory scenes: full-frame 2D action views.
- The animation runs at approximately 60 frames per second and repeats automatically.
